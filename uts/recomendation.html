Basic Skincare Recommendations

Panduan dasar perawatan berdasarkan karakteristik kulit.

Normal Skin

Recommended Care

  • Gentle facial cleanser
  • Lightweight moisturizer
  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen

Dry Skin

Recommended Care

  • Gentle hydrating cleanser
  • Moisturizer with ceramides
  • Moisturizing sunscreen

Oily Skin

Recommended Care

  • Gentle facial cleanser
  • Lightweight moisturizer
  • Non-comedogenic sunscreen

Combination Skin

Recommended Care

  • Gentle facial cleanser
  • Lightweight moisturizer
  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen

Important Note

Setiap kulit memiliki kebutuhan yang berbeda. Pilih produk sesuai kondisi kulit dan perhatikan reaksi kulit ketika mencoba produk baru.
